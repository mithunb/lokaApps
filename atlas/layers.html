Add your data to the mapExperimental

Every atlas starts with its default open-data layers — this is where you make it yours. Drop in your data — survey results by village, a facility list, field GPS points — and walk it onto the map in four steps: check the table we read, place the rows, preview the styled layer on the real map, then add it. Nothing touches your atlas until the last step.
