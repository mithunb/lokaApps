Fork D — what is left of "Change"

Your note "Remove the change control" sits directly under a list of colour removals, so it honestly reads two ways. D1: only the colour block goes, and the card keeps the three jobs that still exist: name the layer, choose what each place is called, remove it. D2: the whole card goes, and those three jobs move into the layer's row. Both are drawn; the jobs must live somewhere, because renaming and removing a layer cannot become impossible.

I would ship D1 — the card stays, three jobs and nothing else. All three are rare, deliberate, owner-only acts, and the card gives them what rare deliberate acts need: room to say what they do, and a calm confirm for the one that destroys. The colour question, its warning, and the themes block all leave — the card drops from seven things to three.
Why not D2 D2 saves one surface but hides three jobs behind a "⋯" symbol, a habit from software for people who live in software. Your owners visit rarely; a door that says Change is found on the first visit, a ⋯ is found by accident. And the destroy action ends up two levels deep in a floating menu.
