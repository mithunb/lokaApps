Start from your data

Drop a file with locations — a CSV or Excel sheet with latitude/longitude or place names, or a GeoJSON / KML / GPX. We read where your rows are and suggest the smallest region that covers them. You confirm, then we build the atlas around it and add your data as the first layer.
